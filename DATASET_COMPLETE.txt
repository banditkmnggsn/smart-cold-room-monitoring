✅ STEP 3: 15 Feature Engineering

   Raw Sensors (4):
   ├─ temperature1_c, humidity1_percent
   ├─ temperature2_c, humidity2_percent
   
   Delta/Rate (4):
   ├─ dT1 = T1_now - T1_prev (detect rate of change)
   ├─ dT2 = T2_now - T2_prev
   ├─ abs_dT1 = |dT1| (magnitude)
   └─ abs_dT2 = |dT2|
   
   Trend/Moving Average (2):
   ├─ avgT1_5 = average of last 5 T1 readings
   └─ avgT2_5 = average of last 5 T2 readings
   
   Noise Detection (2):
   ├─ stdT1_5 = std dev of last 5 T1 readings
   └─ stdT2_5 = std dev of last 5 T2 readings
   
   Sensor Health (3):
   ├─ stuck_count1 = # readings where |dT1| < 0.01°C
   ├─ stuck_count2 = # readings where |dT2| < 0.01°C
   └─ diffT = |T1 - T2| ← MOST IMPORTANT for sensor fault detection!
