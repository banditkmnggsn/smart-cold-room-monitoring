================================================================================
                        DETECTION THRESHOLDS
================================================================================

Normal Operation:
- Temperature range: 6-8.5°C
- Temperature change: dT < 0.2°C
- Sensor offset: diffT < 0.3°C

Cooling Failure:
- Temperature: > 8.5°C
- Temperature rising: dT > +0.2°C
- Sensors agree: diffT < 3.5°C

Sensor Fault:
- Large offset: diffT > 3.5°C
- Unrealistic temperature: T < -15°C or T > +50°C
- Stuck sensor: repeated values > 1000 times
- High noise: stdT > 1.0°C
